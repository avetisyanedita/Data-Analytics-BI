## 📌 Executive Summary

This project delivers an interactive **KPI Performance & Data Quality Reporting solution** designed to help stakeholders monitor KPI health, identify data-quality issues, track reporting trends, and prioritize areas requiring attention.

Built in **Power BI**, the solution transforms KPI and operational reporting data into an executive analytics experience that enables users to monitor performance, compare reporting periods, identify declining areas, and move from high-level portfolio insights to detailed KPI analysis.

The dashboard consists of three analytical views:

- **Executive Summary** — executive-level KPI health, progress status, portfolio overview, and key risk areas.
- **Report Portfolio** — detailed KPI-level performance, variance, and reporting breakdown.
- **Trend Analysis** — period-over-period trends, department breakdowns, and required actions.

---

## 🎯 Stakeholder Objectives

The report is designed to help **business stakeholders monitor KPI performance and identify areas requiring investigation or corrective action**.

The analysis focuses on questions that matter when evaluating KPI health:

- **What is the current KPI landscape?** — Understand the overall reporting portfolio and performance status.
- **Which KPIs require attention?** — Identify declining or underperforming KPIs.
- **Where are data-quality issues concentrated?** — Explore KPI categories, functions, and reporting areas contributing to current issues.
- **How is performance changing?** — Compare current results with previous reporting periods.
- **Which areas are improving?** — Identify KPIs and departments showing positive progress.
- **Where should action be prioritized?** — Highlight key risk areas and required business actions.
- **How can KPI information be explored efficiently?** — Use interactive filtering to move from portfolio-level insights to individual KPI details.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & User Takeaways

- **Clear performance visibility:** The report provides a structured view of KPI health across the reporting portfolio, making performance changes easier to identify.

- **Risk-focused reporting:** Declining KPIs are surfaced prominently, helping stakeholders focus attention on areas requiring investigation.

- **Period-over-period context:** Current performance is compared with previous reporting periods to reveal meaningful changes rather than isolated results.

- **Data-quality visibility:** KPI categories, functions, and reporting areas provide context for understanding where data-quality issues are concentrated.

- **Action-oriented analysis:** The trend analysis view connects performance changes with department-level breakdowns and required actions.
